## Session 46 findings (paddling)

- **GPS-derived structure** (mass-independent): dock 0–14 min; warmup 14.8–30.9
  (1.36 mi @ 5.1 mph); pseudo-TT ~32–42 min — half 1 strong 0.5 mi @ 6.6 mph /
  9 mph peak (~4:33), half 2 faded 0.5 mi @ 5.6 mph (~5:11) = **big positive
  split**; the "break" reads as an ease, not a full stop; long steady side-switch
  block 44–87 (3.58 mi @ 5.0 mph); cooldown + dead stop 87–102.
- **Stroke shape**: Austin's arch is a single front-loaded peak and is **stable**
  (fullness 0.37–0.40) across hard TT / faded TT / cruise — he scales effort by
  **force magnitude** (work 359→300→247 J, peak 146→128→79 lbf), not by changing
  the arch shape.
- **Cross-paddler leg-drive is NOT cleanly comparable in absolutes.** Absolute
  work/peak are boat-response and scale with system mass + hull + blade. Austin's
  easy cruise (247 J) already exceeds the owner's *hardest* session-45 leg piece
  (147 J) — that's mostly heavier rig + different boat/blade, not more leg drive.
  To measure leg drive you need a **within-paddler, same-side, adjacent A/B**
  (legs-quiet vs legs-driving) and compare the work **delta**, so mass/boat/blade
  cancel. See `legdrive-shows-in-work-per-stroke` in memory.
- **Chill-vs-chill is the fair cross-paddler comparison, and the robust
  difference is GLIDE/RUN, not peak.** Effort-matched at low effort, Austin's hull
  barely decelerates between strokes (glide trough ~−6 to −14 lbf) while the owner
  **checks hard** (~−19 lbf DB blade s42, ~−25 to −30 lbf OC blade s45) — same on
  *both* owner blades, so it's recovery/catch technique, not blade/mass/effort.
  Austin's peak also sits **later** (~1.0 m vs owner ~0.75–0.85 m) then releases
  smooth and shallow, vs the owner's earlier peak + deep check.
  **Actionable for the owner: smoother recovery / cleaner catch to cut the
  between-stroke check and hold more run.**
- **Consistency**: Austin's single-side stroke is metronomic (work CV ~5%, peak
  CV ~8% over the pure-left TT), tighter than the owner's tightest piece. His
  mixed warmup only *looked* variable (work CV 27%) because it blends strong-left
  + weaker-right — mixing sides ~5×'s his work CV, an indirect confirmation of an
  L/R divide we can't label directly.
- **Caveat that bit us**: glide-trough *depth scales with speed*, so glide/run
  comparisons are valid ONLY effort-matched.
- **Side auto-detection (yaw envelope) is noise-limited in flat water** — get
  side-per-piece from Austin / Garmin, don't trust KG's side signal here.

## Durable cross-session learnings (the durable stuff)

- **KG and the NK SpeedCoach both detect strokes from hull motion**, not arm
  motion. Only the wrist Garmin senses the arm. KG↔SC count differences are
  algorithmic, not arm-vs-thrust.
- **Leg drive shows up in work-per-stroke** (area under force-vs-distance), not
  in peak force / impulse / connected% / yaw-side-bias. Compare matched adjacent
  same-side pairs; blind whole-lap scans wash it out.
- **Absolute work/force is boat-response, not blade force** — it scales with
  mass, hull, and blade. Never compare absolute work across boats/paddlers/blades;
  only within-paddler deltas or effort-matched shape/glide.
- **KG measures boat thrust; the wrist Garmin measures arm cadence** — expect
  count differences from that alone.
- Alignment: prefer **TIME-record** alignment (firmware ≥ 2026-05-23 emits
  KG_REC_TIME on first GPS valid time then every 5 min); fall back to GPS-speed
  cross-correlation for older logs.
